# Changelog

All notable changes to Signal & Noise Tools are documented here.

This file holds two things only: **`## [Unreleased]`**, the working log that
accumulates across pull requests, and the **current release**. Everything older
lives in [docs/changelog/](docs/changelog/).

A pull request does not bump `Version` and does not tag — it closes an issue and
adds a bullet below. A release is a separate, deliberate act:
`tools/cut-release.sh`.

## [Unreleased]


### Fixed
- **Your devices: Last seen moves on the owner's day.** It moved only when the marker was re-signed, once per UTC day, so a browser enrolled on an evening (already tomorrow in UTC) still read yesterday the next morning (live, 2026-10-16). Last seen now updates on the first wp-admin visit of each site-local day, at most one write a day; the marker's signing day is unchanged, so the Workers verify it as before.
## [2026.10.20] - 2026-10-16 — S&N Home screens lead with their status and keep settings in one Setup card

### Changed
- **Five Connections leaves take the Analytics shape: one status line, a Setup card, a How this works fold.** Discography, Webhooks, Zenodo and Cloudflare each lead with one status line answering "is this fine?" (a problem keeps its notice open beside it); their settings and reference sit in one Setup card of folds whose closed line states the current value and that opens itself when something needs you (Muso.AI profile and Spotify; Uptime monitoring and the payload reference; the Zenodo environment; the Cloudflare keys and grants, which never print a key, not even masked); and each long intro is a closed How this works. Discography's Sync now and Cron's Send test brief, Acknowledge current settings and Run now are compact inline buttons instead of a full-width row each. No figure, link, button, form or field is gone; field names, nonces and actions are unchanged. Cloudways, IndexNow, Credentials and Scheduled were already clean and stay as they were.
- **The Analytics leaf's structure is a kit pattern other leaves can use.** Three helpers in `inc/openstation-kit-anatomy.php`: `snt_kit_status_line()` (one row of chips, a door on the right), `snt_kit_setup_card()` (one card of settings, each a fold whose closed line states its current value) and `snt_kit_how_fold()` (a small closed "How this works" for long explanations). Measurement › Analytics now paints through them, and `docs/kit-leaf-anatomy.md` says when to use each and when not to. The fold guard (`tests/sn-dashboard-no-folds.php`) counts calls to the two folding helpers, so no leaf can fold through them unlisted. `snt_kit_form()` takes `inline` for a compact one-button form.
- **Content and AI leaves take the Analytics shape where it helps.** AI › Models & budget leads with one status line (the spend against the cap, the Workers AI token, a failed billing read), its readings follow in rows, and the settings form closes the leaf in a Setup card: one fold for the whole form whose closed line names the models and the cap, and one for the Workers AI token that opens while it is not set; the intro is a How this works fold. Content › Block migrations and Pattern adoption lead with the count and the scan button on one line, and what the scanner looks for moves into How this works. Content › Tags opens on one status line in place of three stat tiles, and Read tags now is a compact button. Resume's Publish, Discard draft and Revert sit side by side instead of one row each. No figure, link, button, form or field is removed, and every field name, nonce and action is unchanged. Now, Uses, Workflow, Vocabulary, MCP Clients and Agent tools are unchanged: editing surfaces, a pure readout, or already shaped this way.
- **Five more Measurement leaves take the Analytics shape, in the parts each needs.** Insights opens on its working row: its intro is a How this works fold at the foot of Run Analysis, Settings is a Setup card whose Weekly scan line reads On or Off, and Mark done, Snooze and Dismiss are small inline buttons. RSS is one row: Activity leads with a status line (tracking on, or off as a warning, and how long the log is kept), the Setup card under it holds Settings and Maintenance as folds whose lines state the setting, beside Recent requests; Reset to Defaults is a small button. Machine readers' sensor pills are one status line with the two S&N Analytics doors on its right (every warning stays open under it), the Edge sensor readout and Sensor settings are Setup folds that open while unread or tokenless, and the rights-evidence Lift, Hold, Post now and Retract buttons are compact. Search Console's credential form is a Setup fold that stays open until a key parses. Health's Run scan is a compact button under its sentence. Every figure, link, button, form, field name, nonce and action is unchanged. The Tools leaves (Citations, Lanes, Links, Provenance, Reports, Trust checks) keep their layout: each already reads clean.
- **Security and Site leaves take the Analytics layout where it helps.** Login URL leads with one status line (the module's state; a dormant or bypassed module keeps its open warning), the module's explanation moves into How this works, and Emergency unlock becomes a Setup fold beside the slug form whose closed line names the wp-config.php constant that is set (open while one is). Performance leads with one status line (speculative loading on or off, failed outbound calls), the outbound-call ledger is the working content, and the setting and its Profile move into a Setup card (closed lines "On" or "Off" and "prerender, moderate eagerness"); the Speculation Rules intro is How this works. Audit log's retention form is a Setup fold whose closed line is the window ("90 days"). Prune now, Send test digest, a broken path's Dismiss and a redirect's Delete are compact one-button forms instead of full-width rows. Every figure, link, form, field name, nonce and action is unchanged. S&N Home, Front-End, Identity & SEO, Firewall and the Redirects and Broken links ledgers keep their layout.

### Fixed
- **A fold inside a card no longer titles larger than the card.** In the Setup card, "Credentials" painted at 20px under the card's own "Setup". A fold inside any card now titles at 14px, weight 500, on one line ("Configured elsewhere" no longer breaks in two), with its closed summary on the rest of that line, right-aligned, wrapping inside its column instead of running past the card. A fold that is the card itself (Scheduled events, the Lanes and door folds, Connect a client) keeps its size.
- **Your devices is compact.** Each Revoke sat alone on a full-width row with a rule above it as a solid accent button, so the card stood twice its neighbors' height. The Revokes and Revoke all now sit in one wrapping row of small danger-outline buttons under the table, and "This device is counted when logged out." shares its line with its button (now a quiet secondary). Revoke all's long hint moved into How this works; its confirm still states it before anything is revoked. Every form, field name, nonce and action is unchanged.
