# Changelog

All notable changes to Signal & Noise Tools are documented here.

This file holds two things only: **`## [Unreleased]`**, the working log that
accumulates across pull requests, and the **current release**. Everything older
lives in [docs/changelog/](docs/changelog/).

A pull request does not bump `Version` and does not tag — it closes an issue and
adds a bullet below. A release is a separate, deliberate act:
`tools/cut-release.sh`.

## [2026.10.5] - 2026-10-12 — the audit finished: every screen says what it measured, widget links land, and a Signal through noise desktop theme

### Added
- **A desktop theme, "Signal through noise", for the OpenStation desktop** (owner-approved 2026-10-12). One red signal line through gray noise is its only mark. It changes nothing until you pick it in OS Settings, Appearance (the choice is saved to your account), and it leaves your accent, wallpaper, widgets, dock, window corners and every other OS setting as they are.
  - **App icons.** S&N Home (faders), Signal & Noise (a pulse) and S&N Analytics (a level meter) share one family: white strokes on charcoal, one red mark each, in the dock and on the desktop.
  - **The purple and pink fills, edges and glows become signal.** The meshes and holographic films OpenStation paints regardless of your accent go flat: Station Home's and S&N Home's rail (and the card on OS Settings' About tab) turns charcoal with a thin red trace at its edge, a lit control's edge turns red, a resting edge turns a quiet gray, and a hovered row in a dock item's submenu turns light gray with a red marker. Step numbers, ribbons, progress bars and the holographic button turn light gray, and count badges red; their dark labels stay readable (16:1 and 6:1). Focus outlines on title-bar buttons, text selection, avatar and linked-window glows, and the dock-peek border turn red; the tag field's border and the icons in Station Home's and S&N Home's lists turn gray (4.5:1 and 7:1). The outline of an off switch, a range slider's track and an unticked checkbox is at least 3:1 on every surface (3.27:1 to 4.47:1). Two link and accent text colors stay pink: text colors are out of bounds for this theme.
  - **Window titles in Bebas Neue.** Body text, the clock and the big figures stay as they are.
  - **Two wallpapers to pick, never applied for you:** Signal (faint gray noise and one red line with a pulse, drawn once as it loads, still if you have asked for less motion) and Quiet (the noise alone).
