# Stamps and acknowledgements

## As of

Every item carries the time of the reading it came from, printed in the site's timezone (Settings, General) with the zone's abbreviation. "As of 2026-09-14 04:00:28 EDT" means the sweep that produced the item ran at that moment. A newer state of the world is not visible until that reader runs again.

## Acknowledge

Acknowledge hides an item until its stamp changes. It records the stamp, not the item: when the reader produces the same finding with a newer time, the item returns. Acknowledging does not resolve, fix or re-read anything.

## Not stamped

A row that could not say when it was measured says "not stamped". That is a gap in the reader's record, not a claim about the subject.
