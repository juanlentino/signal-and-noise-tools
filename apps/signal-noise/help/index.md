# Signal & Noise

The Signal & Noise app is the site's editorial desk inside OpenStation. It reads the corpus (the notes, the pages, the scheduled queue) against Search Console, the provenance ledger, the link graph and the health scan, and lists what needs a hand.

Sections:

- [Attention](attention.md): the queue of readings that need a person.
- [Readers](readers.md): the ten sources the queue is composed from, and what each one measures.
- [Stamps and acknowledgements](stamps.md): what "as of" means, and what Acknowledge does.
- [S&N Home and Analytics](windows.md): the two native windows the app opens doors into.

Everything the app shows is a reading with a time on it. Nothing here runs a scan, a sweep or a sync; those run on their own schedules and the app reads what they stored.
