**Goal:** three sections beside Notes and Discography — Pages (signed pages, a `post`-kind section with the dossier, the menu and drag-out), Citations and Scheduled fragments (read-only entries with doors) — with phase one and two unchanged for Notes.

**Decided facts (from the map, 2026-09-06):**
1. The registry reads `id, label, items, capability, position` (inc/openstation-app.php:94-120; `items` must be callable or the descriptor is silently dropped); the payload reads `count, icon, kind, statuses, default_status, edit_url, restPath, columns` (parts/payload.php:50-84). Without `count`, `items()` runs at the ROOT on every paint.
2. An entry item paints from `id, title, subtitle, thumbnail, icon, status, statusLabel, date, dateLabel, badge{text,tone,title}|null, columns{}, detail{hero,facts[[l,v]],blocks[],actions[]}` (parts/discography.php:90-108); blocks are table|code|text; actions are `{label,url}` or `{label,variant?,dispatch,args}`.
3. The client gates the control surface on the descriptor (`'post' === section.kind && section.restPath`, client:68) but the DOSSIER on the literal `section.id === 'notes'` (client:885, :985; pinned as a count of 2 in tests/openstation-app.php:130 and tests/openstation-app-client.php:57) and the DRAG on `DRAG_ENTITY = { notes: 'signal-noise:notes' }` (client:57, :1094-1098; pinned at tests/openstation-app-client.php:72).
4. `snt_os_app_note_provenance()` refuses a page (`! sn_prov_is_note()`, inc/openstation-app.php:151) and is the only source of the badge, the versions column, the chain table and the UID block. `sn_prov_subject_kind()` (inc/provenance-core.php:624-644) answers `note | page | ''`; the ledger and uid helpers are type-agnostic; `sn_prov_ledger_dir( 'page' )` is `pages/`.
5. The dossier refuses a page in ONE place: `sn_note_dossier_post()` (inc/note-dossier.php:54-60). The trust builder already resolves the kind (-trust.php:83-86). Editorial reads `post_tag` (pages have none) and `snt_ml_related_for_post()` (the kernel indexes notes).
6. A page opts into signing through `SN_PROV_SIGN_META = '_sn_prov_sign'` (registered for `page` only). No signed-pages query exists; the UID query precedent is inc/provenance-admin.php:22-28.
7. `parts/notes.php` assumes a post in fourteen places (map §1): `post_type`, the five statuses, `category_name`, the provenance summary, the `/verify/` link, `publish_posts`, the thumbnail, the icon, `notes_edit_url()`'s type check. `note_allowed()` gates on `'post'` and `publish_posts` (parts/actions.php:76, :86).
8. Citations: rows are stdClass with a stable `id`; no list-all function (the leaf inlines `LIMIT 100`, inc/citations-admin.php:217); tiers `SN_CIT_TIERS`; `sn_cit_tier_pill_kind()` → `ok|warn|muted|''`, bridged by `sn_note_dossier_tone()`; `sn_cit_ago_label()`; door `snt_desktop_admin_url( 'sn-tools', 'citations' )`.
9. Schedules: `sn_schedule_all()` is unbounded, `id ASC`, ARRAY_A; columns per map §3; statuses `queued|active|done|error` as literals with no constant; `target_ref` is the post id AS A STRING with `target_type === 'fragment'`; `sn_admin_schedule_fmt_gmt()`; door `snt_desktop_admin_url( 'sn-connections', 'scheduled-content' )`. The leaf's three operations are gated in the admin-post door, not in their functions: a later phase calls the engine directly and re-asserts `manage_options`.
10. The `WP_Query` stub in tests/openstation-app.php:91-94 ignores its args and returns `$GLOBALS['__posts']` for every query.

**Decisions on the map's fifteen unknowns:**
- (1) A signed page is one with `SN_PROV_SIGN_META` truthy: opted in, whether or not a version exists yet (its dossier then says "no signed version"). Query: `post_type => 'page'`, the same five statuses, `meta_key => SN_PROV_SIGN_META`, `meta_value => '1'`, `'perm' => 'readable'`.
- (2) `snt_os_app_note_provenance()` widens its gate to `'' !== sn_prov_subject_kind( $post )`; a note still resolves to `note`. Move the two pins (tests/openstation-app.php:163-164, :173-174) to the new gate's wording.
- (3) The dossier is gated on a DESCRIPTOR field `hasDossier` (true for Notes and Pages), projected by payload.php into `section.hasDossier`; the client's two id literals become `section.hasDossier` in both places; the two count pins move from the literal to `section.hasDossier` (count 2). The `updated()` fetch stays keyed on `state.item`.
- (4) `DRAG_ENTITY` gains `pages: 'signal-noise:pages'`; the pin at tests/openstation-app-client.php:72 gains the second key. The Trash target accepts page shortcuts (map: it refuses only `user` and `attachment`).
- (5) Publish and its right derive from the post type object: `get_post_type_object( $post->post_type )->cap->publish_posts` (which is `publish_pages` for a page); `edit_post`/`delete_post` are meta caps and already map per type.
- (6) The Pages section's `capability` is `edit_pages`; BOTH the Notes and the Pages queries add `'perm' => 'readable'`, so an Author sees only their own unpublished posts of either type. The policy doc gains one sentence recording this.
- (7) The `/verify/` action is built only when the subject kind is `note`; a page's ledger record stays reachable through the dossier's anchor-proof door. Recorded as an amendment until the theme's verifier is confirmed to accept a page.
- (8) Editorial: the Tags tile and block are omitted when `! is_object_in_taxonomy( $post->post_type, 'post_tag' )`; the Related block is omitted when the subject kind is not `note` (the kernel indexes notes). Both said in the builder's docblock, neither painted as "Untagged" or "not in the index".
- (9) Add `sn_cit_all( $limit )` to inc/citations-store.php (`ORDER BY first_seen_gmt DESC LIMIT %d`, objects); the leaf keeps its own query untouched in this phase.
- (10) The Scheduled section lists FRAGMENTS only (`target_type === 'fragment'`), sorted soonest-first by `starts_at` (UTC) with empty starts last, then `id`; it does not fold in scheduled posts.
- (11) Both entry sections supply a `count`: Citations sums the four tiers of `sn_cit_counts()`; Scheduled uses a new `sn_schedule_count( $target_type )` (`SELECT COUNT(*) … WHERE target_type = %s`) in inc/schedule-engine.php.
- (12) The `WP_Query` stub honours `post_type` (string or array) and, when `meta_key` is given, `$GLOBALS['__meta'][ id ][ key ]` against `meta_value`; negative-controlled by asserting the Notes fixtures do NOT come back for the pages query.
- (13) `SN_SCHEDULE_STATUSES = array( 'queued', 'active', 'done', 'error' )` is added to inc/schedule-engine.php beside the table constant and used by the section's status pills; the engine's own literals are left for a later refactor (out of scope, noted in the CHANGELOG).
- (14) Positions: Notes 10, Pages 12, Discography 20, Citations 30, Scheduled 40. The test's foreign section stays at 15; the order pins move to `notes, pages, ledger, discography, citations, schedules` where the fixture registers all.
- (15) Citations and Scheduled register ALWAYS (a measured zero is honest); Discography keeps its own hide-when-empty rule.

**House rules:** as phase two's plan (never commit; touch only your files; test conventions; stub-parity; no orphan classes; ASCII in CI).
